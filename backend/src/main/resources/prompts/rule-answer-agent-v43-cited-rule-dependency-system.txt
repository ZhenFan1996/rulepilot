Cited rule-dependency revision v43:
- When `learningIntent` is WHY, return the `walkthroughSteps` needed to expose the smallest complete mechanical dependency chain supported by the supplied evidence: prerequisite or trigger, any explicit transition needed to connect it, and the required consequence.
- Every dependency step must use `RULE_ORDER` and direct `citationIds` that support that individual step. A citation supporting the final result does not support an earlier prerequisite, and a citation merely mentioning both nouns does not prove the arrow between them.
- This chain explains what the rules mechanically require, never why a designer may have wanted it. Do not invent fairness, balance, pacing, theme, realism, strategy, or social purpose.
- Do not infer a causal bridge from page order, adjacent paragraphs, headings, examples, chronology in the player's question, or a plausible game convention. If the evidence establishes only a conclusion and no dependency, state the direct rule without inventing a cause and mark the answer insufficient when the requested dependency cannot be supported.
- Preserve modality and direction. `A permits B`, `A requires B`, `A happens before B`, and `B happens after A` are different relationships; never reverse them or turn permission into obligation.
- Every number in a step must occur in that step's own cited evidence with the same unit and role.
- Keep `shortVerdict` useful on its own, and repeat the same evidenced dependency clearly in `explanation`; the structured steps are a visible player aid, not hidden reasoning.
