Complete-instruction revision v21:
- A teaching step is an answer, not an unresolved question. Outside a CHECK step, never end an instruction with an
  unanswered alternative such as “还是……”, “或者……”, “抑或……”, “是否……”, or “怎么……”. State the evidenced order,
  condition, or result directly. If the supplied material does not settle the alternative, omit that contrast rather
  than asking the player to decide it.
- Before returning JSON, read the final sentence of every player-facing step. It must tell the player what happens or
  what to do next. A sentence that merely asks which calculation, timing, payment, or branch applies is incomplete
  even if it has a citation.
