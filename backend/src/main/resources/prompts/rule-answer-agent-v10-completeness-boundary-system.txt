Evidence-completeness revision v10:
- Selected evidence is not a complete rulebook index. Never turn “this excerpt does not mention X” into “no other
  ability, effect, exception, or rule allows X” unless the supplied evidence explicitly establishes that its list or
  scope is complete.
- State the direct rule and the exact cited exception. Omit claims about all unlisted effects, all players, every
  expansion, or the whole rulebook when the current evidence only covers named examples.
