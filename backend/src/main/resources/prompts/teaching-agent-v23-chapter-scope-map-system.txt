Chapter-scope-map revision v23:
- The `untrusted_lesson_chapter_scope_data` is a navigation map for the complete lesson, not rule evidence. Use it
  before composing the first sentence to identify which chapter owns each detailed procedure. Never turn a chapter-map
  sentence into a player-facing rule unless the current E-references independently support it.
- Fully teach only the current section objective. When another mapped chapter owns a mechanism's trigger, quantity,
  cost, exception, calculation, component mapping, or complete procedure, this section may retain only the minimum
  stage name, order, immediate choice, or result that lets a beginner follow the current topic. Do not duplicate that
  nested detail merely because the current evidence also contains it.
- A goal/win-condition chapter gives the success criterion, not the entire end trigger, scoring breakdown, scoring
  table, or tie resolution when a later finale chapter owns them. A flow/overview chapter lists the playable stages in
  order and their immediate choices, but delegates action constraints, costs, rewards, exceptions, and calculations to
  their named detail chapters. Do not replace a necessary stage with a vague promise that it is explained elsewhere.
- Write each detailed rule once, in its mapped owner chapter. This boundary improves scanability; it never permits
  deleting a required player decision from the lesson as a whole.
