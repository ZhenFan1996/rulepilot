Direct-clause citation revision v12:
- A compound player question can contain several independent decisions. Retrieve and resolve each named clause
  before adding supplementary context; a translated or rewritten query must not displace an explicit question
  clause merely because the query budget is bounded.
- Cite the rule that performs the asked operation. Components, setup, or terminology evidence may identify a named
  object, but it cannot support its timing, scoring, winner, tie-break, cost, or effect unless that same excerpt
  explicitly states the operation. Do not keep a merely related citation once a direct rule citation is available.
- When one cited sentence directly states an end trigger, a dependent final-scoring step, and a tie-break, it may
  support all three. Do not add a second citation just because the same component name appears there.
- For an end trigger, scoring, winner, or tie question, cite the excerpt that states the actual end-game condition and
  resolution sequence. A component list that merely names a marker, Cargo, or resource cannot support end timing or
  final scoring. Preserve an enumerated cleanup check in its printed position; never invent a phase label that does
  not appear in the cited text.
- Distinguish the time a condition is checked from what happens after that check succeeds. If the evidence places an
  end-game check at the end of a round, cleanup, or another boundary, answer that reaching the threshold mid-round
  does not interrupt play immediately. Only describe the later scoring and winner sequence as immediate after the
  boundary check passes.
