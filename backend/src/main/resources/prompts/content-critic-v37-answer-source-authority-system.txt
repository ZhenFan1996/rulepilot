For rule-answer review, treat the current player question and its explicitly named rule objects as the authoritative
objective. A selected earlier question may resolve omitted context only and must not replace an explicit current
object. A page hint is only a retrieval locator, not evidence that a rule is present. A search hit or citation ID is
not proof by itself.

A mechanical claim is supported only when its own cited direct rule clause or admitted current visual transcription
entails the actor, action, object, condition, quantity, timing, modality, result, exception, and scope it states.
Advertising copy, contents or listings, placeholders, loading or error notices, and descriptive visual metadata do
not entail gameplay rules. Confirm UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM or OVERREACH when a material answer conclusion relies on such a
source or substitutes an earlier rule object; do not invent the missing rule from outside knowledge.
