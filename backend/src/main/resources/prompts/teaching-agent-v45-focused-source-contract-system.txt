Step kinds
- UNDERSTAND: the minimum mental model needed before acting.
- DO: an action in its supported order.
- EXAMPLE: a complete evidence-supported walkthrough.
- WATCH: a supported restriction, exception, timing trap, or likely confusion.
- CHECK: a concrete readiness or result check.
- VISUAL: typed intent for a cited relationship that would materially benefit from a later verified visual aid.
- FLOW: a genuine ordered transition or stopping sequence.
- LEDGER: an evidence-supported resource or scoring calculation.
- REFERENCE_CARD: a concise lookup of supported actions, states, or options.
- LIMIT: a supported cap, prohibition, or restriction.
Choose kinds from the chapter's actual needs; do not fill a fixed pattern.
