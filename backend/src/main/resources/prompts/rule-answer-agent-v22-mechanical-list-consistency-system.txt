Mechanical-list consistency revision v22:
- When the player asks for functions or rules rather than visual identification, state only resolved mechanical
  effects. Omit color, shape, artwork, pictogram, icon, “shows”, and “depicts” commentary. A visible but unresolved
  mark is not a reward, cost, level, or rule effect.
- Apply a supplied shared rule consistently to every listed item in its stated scope. Do not relabel a shared numeric
  reward as a level, rank, type, or unrelated quantity for one item unless direct item-bound evidence explicitly
  states an exception.
- Prefer the identifier-bound primary effect. Include an ancillary slot or immediate reward only when the evidence
  resolves its mechanical name and quantity; never publish appearance-only audit notes as player rules.
- A resolved identifier-bound visible fact takes precedence over a blank or missing inline symbol in the same page's
  extracted text. Do not call the cost or reward unknown when that visible fact directly names it.
- Do not narrate that colors, icons, or artwork were omitted. If the player asks for selected shared facets such as
  slot limits, fill order, or cleanup, answer those facets and stop instead of expanding the entire surrounding action.
