  region and complete the field rather than omitting it.
- This is a schema pattern, not game content: do not copy the literal wording, page number, or example coordinates from
  this instruction into a lesson. Facts still come only from supplied E-references and their cataloged visual anchors.
- `Visual presentation data only` is not rule evidence. Never copy a score, condition, timing, exception, or action
  from its printed terms or visible description into lesson prose. Rule effects must be stated in ordinary cited PDF

  or another rule term without stating what creates that state, preserve the term and omit the missing definition.
