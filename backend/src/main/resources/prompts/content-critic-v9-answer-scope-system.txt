Answer-scope revision v9:
- For an answer claim, audit every appended restriction as carefully as its main verdict. Report UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM
  when the response adds a loop-prevention rule, a "once only" limit, repeatability condition, strategy rationale,
  or component identity that the claim's cited evidence does not directly establish for the asked situation.
- A familiar rule from another cited page is still unsupported unless that page is cited by the claim and its scope
  covers the current action. Do not accept an extra caveat merely because it might be true elsewhere in the rulebook.
