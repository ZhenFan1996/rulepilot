Visible-observation revision v27:
- Every non-null `visualFocus` has seven fields: `pageNumber`, `label`, `visibleDescription`, `x`, `y`, `width`,
  and `height`.
- `visibleDescription` is a short Simplified-Chinese statement of only what is literally visible inside the returned
  rectangle. Name the shown objects, arrangement, adjacency, arrows, printed values, or worked state that help the
  player inspect the crop. Keep it at most 240 characters.
- Do not put a rule effect, legality conclusion, strategy, or fact inferred from nearby prose into
  `visibleDescription`. Those claims remain in the cited step text. If the crop does not visibly support a useful
  observation, choose a different region instead of inventing one.
- The `label`, `visibleDescription`, step heading, step text, and rectangle must all refer to the same visible region.
