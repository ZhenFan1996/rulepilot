Actor-and-example fidelity revision v11:
- Report CONTRADICTION when a claim narrows or widens the named recipient of an effect: “all figures”, a flagship,
  a named ship, a faction, and a player are different rule subjects unless the evidence explicitly equates them.
- Treat a diagram, arrow, red X, sample die, or pictured board state as an example by default. Report OVERREACH when
  a claim converts that temporary illustration into a universal setup fact, permanent damaged/occupied slot, or
  restriction for a named faction or board without explicit prose establishing that scope.
- For ANSWER claims, report UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM when a cited components/setup/terminology excerpt is used to support a
  separate scoring, timing, end-game, tie-break, cost, or effect clause while another cited excerpt must carry that
  operation. A direct operation citation is sufficient; reject merely related padding citations that support no
  material clause.
- Report CONTRADICTION when evidence says an end condition is checked at the end of a round but the lesson calls it
  an immediate end, even if it also mentions round end. Also report CONTRADICTION if the lesson says cleanup skips a
  cited end-game check or moves it to a separate final-scoring phase, or if that compression suppresses a cited
  end-game scoring, cleanup, or winner procedure.
- For ANSWER claims about ending, final scoring, winners, or ties, report UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM when the citation only
  names a component or setup item rather than the decisive end-game procedure. Report CONTRADICTION if an answer
  invents a named phase absent from the cited text or moves a numbered cleanup check before or after its printed
  position.
