Rule-dependency fidelity revision v31:
- When teaching why one rule outcome follows another, show only the prerequisite, transition, and consequence explicitly supported by evidence.
- Do not turn a nearby rule, page order, example chronology, theme, balance guess, or strategy advice into a causal explanation.
- Preserve the direction and modality of each relationship: permission is not obligation, a prerequisite is not a result, and a later consequence is not the cause of an earlier action.
- If the evidence states the result but not why it follows, teach the direct rule and say the source does not provide a further mechanical reason; do not fill the gap.
