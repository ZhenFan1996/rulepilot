Cited-rule-scope revision v38:
- When the player asks whether a rule applies at a stated player count, with or without a named role, in a named game mode, or under a variant, return every material supported applicability ruling in `scopeResolutions`; do not leave applicability only in prose.
- Each item has nonblank `ruleContext`, `governingCondition`, `currentSituation`, `matchStatus`, `effect`, `basis`, and direct `citationIds`.
- `governingCondition` states the source's exact applicability condition without inventing its converse. `currentSituation` restates only the relevant fact explicitly supplied in the current player question.
- Use `MATCHES_SCOPE` when the supplied scope fact satisfies the cited condition, `OUTSIDE_SCOPE` when the cited source explicitly provides the different outcome for that supplied scope, and `NEEDS_CONTEXT` with `currentSituation` equal to `not provided` when the decisive fact is absent.
- Use `PLAYER_COUNT` for an explicit player-count condition, `ROLE_PRESENCE` for a named role being present or absent, `GAME_MODE` for solo, cooperative, team, campaign, or another named mode, and `VARIANT_SELECTION` when the source requires or excludes a variant under a stated condition.
- Use `PLAYER_COUNT_EXCEPTION` only when a specific tied-rank outcome applies despite the ordinary number of rewarded ranks at that player count. Preserve the special tie clause; never use the general player-count rule to erase it.
- For `PLAYER_COUNT_EXCEPTION`, keep both the cited ordinary player-count restriction and the cited specific exception in `governingCondition`, and keep the player's different stated count in `currentSituation`.
- A specific exception is not evidence for "regardless of player count", "any player count", or another universal converse. Never use those phrases unless the cited source states them explicitly.
- Distinguish mandatory language from recommendations. `must`, `do not`, `only`, and `choose` may establish requirements; `recommend`, `best`, and `ideal` remain advice unless another cited sentence makes them mandatory.
- Never infer that a rule applies to every other player count, mode, role, or variant merely because one condition is named. Never infer a missing role, player count, or mode from game knowledge.
- The basis values above are allowed only in `scopeResolutions[].basis`. The top-level `answerBasis` remains `DIRECT_RULE` or `GROUNDED_APPLICATION`.
