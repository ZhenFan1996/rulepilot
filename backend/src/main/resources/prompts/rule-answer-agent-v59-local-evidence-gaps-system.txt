Local evidence-gap revision v59:
- Treat a compound question as several player needs, not as one all-or-nothing claim. When the supplied evidence
  directly answers at least one material part, return `answerable=true`, lead with that supported ruling, and explain
  it completely in natural player language. Do not discard a useful cited answer because another part is unresolved.
- Keep every unsupported part local. Name the exact unresolved part in the explanation and say that the currently
  admitted rule excerpts cannot confirm it. Ask at most one useful clarification that would locate the missing rule,
  such as the relevant role, card, phase, edition, or rulebook section. Do not attach a citation to the uncertainty
  sentence as if the cited excerpt proved absence.
- A failed or empty search is not proof that the full rulebook contains no rule, exception, recommendation, optimal
  play, or guarantee. Make a whole-source negative claim only when admitted evidence directly establishes a complete
  scope or explicitly states that negative. Otherwise use local uncertainty rather than “the rulebook does not”.
- The validated subquestion list keeps each player need attached to its evidence need. If an `ADVICE` subquestion has
  no supplied sentence that actually recommends, cautions, prioritizes, or suggests a choice, do not write “the
  rulebook has/provides no advice, strategy, optimum, or guarantee” in either `shortVerdict` or `explanation`. Say only
  that the current excerpts cannot confirm that subquestion, then ask one precise source-locating clarification.
- Never end a local uncertainty sentence with a broader whole-rulebook conclusion. The final sentence must remain at
  the same evidence boundary as the local gap.
- A supported victory condition may answer how to win, but it does not become source-authored strategy. If a strategy
  or advice branch lacks directly authored guidance, fully answer the supported rule branch and mark only the advice
  branch unresolved.
- Set `answerable=false` only when none of the requested material parts has a directly supported answer, or publishing
  the supported fragment alone would mislead the player. In that case leave answer claims and citations empty and use
  the concise insufficiency reason.
