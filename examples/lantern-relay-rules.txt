=== PAGE 1 ===
LANTERN RELAY
OBJECTIVE AND HOW TO WIN
Lantern Relay is a race to light the harbor before the fifth round ends.
The player with the most victory points after final scoring wins the game.
To win, a player must accomplish the highest final score.
During the game, focus on lighting docks while preserving cards and glass.

COMPONENTS
The following pieces and cards are included in the box:
1 harbor board, 24 lantern cards, 20 glass tokens,
12 boat markers, 4 score markers, 1 first-player beacon, and 1 round marker.
Lantern cards show a color and a value from one to three.
The board holds docks, tokens pay costs, cards light docks, boats show position,
score markers track points, and the beacon identifies the first player.

=== PAGE 2 ===
SETUP - HOW TO SET UP THE GAME BEFORE PLAYING
1. Place the harbor board in the center and the round marker on round one.
2. Shuffle the lantern cards and place them face down beside the board.
3. Give each player one boat, one score marker, and two glass tokens.
4. Put every score marker on zero and every boat at the harbor entrance.
5. Deal three lantern cards to each player. Keep hands secret.
6. Give the first-player beacon to the player who most recently saw a lighthouse.

=== PAGE 3 ===
ROUND STRUCTURE AND PHASES
A complete round has a Dawn phase, an Action phase, and a Harbor phase.
During Dawn, reveal one lantern card and add its value to the public light row.
During the Action phase, players take one turn clockwise from the beacon holder.
During the Harbor phase, score completed docks, pass the beacon clockwise,
and advance the round marker. After round five, proceed to the game end.
The next round begins after Harbor ends and the round marker advances.

ACTIONS
On a turn, the active player must take exactly one action:
- Sail: pay one glass token and move the boat up to two spaces.
- Light: play one matching lantern card at the boat's current dock.
- Trade: discard two cards, then draw two cards.
Resolve all costs before applying an action's effect. A player may not spend
glass they do not have or move through a dock occupied by another boat.

=== PAGE 4 ===
GAME END AND SCORING
The game end is triggered after the Harbor phase of round five, or immediately
when all six docks are lit. Finish the current round before final scoring.
Score three victory points for each dock you lit, one point for each remaining
glass token, and the printed value of each lantern card still in your hand.
Lose two points for each unlit dock adjacent to your boat.

TIE BREAKER
In case of a tie, the tied player who lit more docks wins. If still tied,
the tied player with more glass wins. If still tied, share the victory.

=== PAGE 5 ===
FIRST ROUND EXAMPLE
Mira reveals a value-two lantern at Dawn. She spends one glass to Sail two
spaces. Tao plays a matching lantern to Light the next dock. At Harbor,
Tao scores that dock, then the beacon and round marker advance.

COMMON MISTAKES
Do not score a dock during the Action phase. Pay every cost before moving.
Do not skip the Harbor phase when the sixth dock is lit during an action.

RECAP
Set up the board, cards, pieces, and starting resources. Each round resolves
Dawn, one action per player, then Harbor. After the end condition, score docks,
glass, cards, penalties, and finally apply both tie breakers in order.
